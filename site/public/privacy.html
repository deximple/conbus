What this site stores

This page exists to say exactly what the waitlist keeps. There is no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party anything.

If you join the waitlist

One row per signup holds:

  • the day you signed up (UTC day only — no time of day)
  • your email
  • your answers: team size, the vendors you checked, whether you run agents unattended, and the optional incident note you wrote
  • which price variant the page showed you and whether you came from the hero or the pricing section
  • that you consented to be contacted

Why: to count real interest by team profile and to write back when a pilot exists. The variant is a fake-door test — it says which price was shown, nothing else.

If you click "Request a pilot"

One counter goes up: the UTC day and the variant shown. Nothing else is recorded — no page views, no session, no identity.

What is never stored

  • No timestamp more precise than the UTC day
  • No IP address
  • No user agent, referrer or browser fingerprint
  • No cookies or other identifiers on your machine

Retention and deletion

Rows are kept until the operator deletes them, and at most 12 months. To have your signup removed, ask at the contact address shown with this site and it is gone.
